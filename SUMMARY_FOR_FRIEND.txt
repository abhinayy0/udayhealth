═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  🆘 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: Website looks broken
Solution: Upload ALL files (index.html, styles.css, script.js, 
          images folder, admin.html)

Problem: Changes not showing
Solution: Clear browser cache (Ctrl+F5) and wait 2 minutes

Problem: Can't download from admin.html
Solution: Run "make serve" first, then open 
          http://localhost:8000/admin.html

Problem: Lost original files
Solution: Download from your hosting service
