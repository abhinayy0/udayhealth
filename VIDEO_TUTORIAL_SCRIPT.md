# 🎥 Video Tutorial Script - For Recording/Teaching

## 📹 5-Minute Tutorial: How to Update Your Website

Use this script to create a video tutorial or teach your friend in person.

---

## 🎬 PART 1: Introduction (30 seconds)

**[Show website on screen]**

"Hello! Today I'll show you how to update your clinic website content in just 3 minutes, without any coding knowledge. You don't need to be technical, and it's completely free!"

---

## 🎬 PART 2: Opening the Editor (1 minute)

**[Show desktop/folder]**

"First, locate the `admin.html` file in your website folder."

**[Right-click on admin.html]**

"Right-click on it, and select 'Open with Chrome' or 'Open with Firefox'."

**[Browser opens with admin.html]**

"Great! This is your content editor. See how simple it looks? Just forms to fill in!"

**[Scroll through the page]**

"You can edit:
- Phone numbers
- Address
- Doctor information
- Hero section text
- Social media links
- Everything in both English and Hindi!"

---

## 🎬 PART 3: Editing Content (1.5 minutes)

**[Click on a phone number field]**

"Let's change a phone number. I'll click here..."

**[Type new number]**

"...and type the new number. See? Just like filling a form!"

**[Scroll to doctor section]**

"Now let's update the doctor's description."

**[Edit the text]**

"I can edit both English and Hindi versions. Just type naturally."

**[Show other fields]**

"You can change any text you see here. The labels tell you what each field is for."

---

## 🎬 PART 4: Saving Changes (30 seconds)

**[Scroll to bottom]**

"Once you're done editing, scroll down..."

**[Click 'Save Changes' button]**

"...and click 'Save Changes'."

**[Show success message]**

"Perfect! You'll see a green success message. Your changes are saved in your browser."

---

## 🎬 PART 5: Downloading Updated File (30 seconds)

**[Click 'Download Updated Website' button]**

"Now click 'Download Updated Website'."

**[Show download notification]**

"The updated index.html file will download to your computer."

**[Show downloaded file]**

"Here it is! This file has all your changes."

---

## 🎬 PART 6: Uploading to Netlify (1.5 minutes)

**[Open new browser tab]**

"Now let's put this online. Open a new tab and go to..."

**[Type in address bar]**

"...app.netlify.com/drop"

**[Show Netlify Drop page]**

"This is Netlify Drop. It's completely free and you don't need an account!"

**[Show file explorer]**

"Open your website folder..."

**[Select all files]**

"...select ALL files - index.html, styles.css, script.js, images folder, and admin.html."

**[Drag files to Netlify]**

"Now just drag them to this box."

**[Show upload progress]**

"Wait a few seconds while it uploads..."

**[Show generated URL]**

"Done! Here's your website URL. Copy this and save it somewhere safe."

**[Click on the URL]**

"Let's check it out..."

**[Website opens]**

"Perfect! Your website is live with all your changes!"

---

## 🎬 PART 7: Future Updates (30 seconds)

**[Show the process quickly]**

"To update in the future:
1. Open admin.html
2. Edit text
3. Save changes
4. Download
5. Go to your Netlify URL
6. Drag the new index.html file
7. Done!"

**[Show timer]**

"This takes just 2-3 minutes once you know the process!"

---

## 🎬 PART 8: Conclusion (30 seconds)

**[Show final website]**

"And that's it! You now have a professional website that you can update anytime, without coding, and completely free!"

**[Show admin.html again]**

"Remember: admin.html is your friend. Bookmark it, and updating your website will always be this easy!"

**[Show contact info]**

"If you have questions, check the HOW_TO_UPDATE_CONTENT.md file for detailed instructions. Happy updating!"

---

## 📝 Key Points to Emphasize

While recording or teaching, emphasize:

1. **"No coding required"** - Repeat this multiple times
2. **"Completely free"** - Mention the zero cost
3. **"Just 2-3 minutes"** - Emphasize the speed
4. **"Like filling a form"** - Make it relatable
5. **"Can't break anything"** - Remove fear

---

## 🎯 Common Questions to Address

### Q: "What if I make a mistake?"
**A:** "Just edit again and re-upload. You can't break anything!"

### Q: "Do I need to pay for hosting?"
**A:** "No! Netlify Drop is completely free forever."

### Q: "What if I forget the steps?"
**A:** "Check the QUICK_START.md file - it has a simple checklist!"

### Q: "Can I edit from my phone?"
**A:** "Yes! You can use mobile apps or even mobile browser."

### Q: "What if the website looks broken?"
**A:** "Make sure you upload ALL files, not just index.html."

## 🎤 Tone & Style

- **Friendly and encouraging**
- **Slow and clear speech**
- **Pause after each step**
- **Show, don't just tell**
- **Smile and be positive**
- **Use simple language**
- **Avoid technical jargon**

---

## 📹 Recording Tips

1. **Use screen recording software:**
   - Windows: Xbox Game Bar (Win+G)
   - Mac: QuickTime Player
   - Free: OBS Studio

2. **Record in 1080p** for clarity

3. **Use a good microphone** or quiet room

4. **Edit out mistakes** - it's okay to re-record

5. **Add text overlays** for key points

6. **Include timestamps** in description
