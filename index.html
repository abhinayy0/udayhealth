Meet Our Expert

Dr. Navneet Agarwal

Neurologist (MBBS, MD)

Dr. Navneet Agarwal is a highly experienced and renowned neurologist from Agra with extensive expertise in treating neurological disorders. With years of dedicated practice, Dr. Agarwal specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions such as Parkinson's disease, epilepsy, migraine, stroke, and other neurological ailments.

Specializations:

  • Parkinson's Disease Treatment
  • Epilepsy Management
  • Migraine & Headache Treatment
  • Stroke Care & Rehabilitation
